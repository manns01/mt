About Me

Hi, I’m Manish Tamta. Raised in Uttarakhand, I am currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Physics at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bangalore. I have also worked as a visiting researcher at the Max Planck Institute for Gravitational Physics (Albert Einstein Institute), Potsdam, Germany.

I hold an M.S. in Physics from IISc and a B.Sc. in Physics from St. Stephen’s College, University of Delhi. My research interests include astroparticle physics, cosmology, gravitational lensing, and dark matter. I work extensively with numerical simulations and am increasingly exploring machine learning and deep learning applications in physics.

Beyond academia, I enjoy traveling and photography as ways to explore and capture new perspectives. I welcome interdisciplinary discussions and collaborations that spark original and impactful ideas — feel free to reach out!

Research Highlights

  • Primordial Black Hole Dark Matter — Exploring microlensing signatures in X-rays to constrain dark matter candidates.
  • Gravitational Lensing Simulations — Developing numerical models to study lensing effects in cosmology.
